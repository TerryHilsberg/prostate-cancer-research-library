# Risk Stratification & Staging

## Scope

Research notes and filed articles for **risk stratification & staging**.

## Key patient questions

- What does this evidence help a patient understand?
- Which risk group or clinical situation does it apply to?
- What are the benefits, harms, and uncertainties?
- What should be discussed with a qualified clinician?

## Filed items

- [2026-08-24 weekly scan](../updates/2026-08-24.md) — Risk/staging watch: a prospective Node-RADS study suggests structured MRI nodal scoring may complement, not replace, validated nomograms and PSMA PET/CT when planning prostatectomy and lymph-node management.
- [2026-08-17 weekly scan](../updates/2026-08-17.md) — Risk/staging watch: a 3,142-case node-staged prostatectomy cohort links lymph-node positivity with Grade Group, tumor volume, PSAD, pT3 stage, and posterior/extensive bilateral topography; MRI-targeted plus systematic biopsy data also reinforce that biopsy approach can affect Grade Group and tumor-burden estimates.
- [AI and Integrated Test Decision Support in Prostate Cancer: Evidence Map](../papers/ai-integrated-prostate-cancer-decision-support-evidence/index.md) — integrated decision-support approaches combining PSA, clinical variables, mpMRI, biopsy/pathology, genomic or blood biomarkers, and PSMA PET/CT for clinically significant cancer risk, staging, treatment intensity, and recurrence/metastasis prediction.
- [PSMA PET/CT in Prostate Cancer: Evidence Map](../papers/psma-pet-ct-prostate-cancer-evidence/index.md) — evidence summary focused on high-risk staging before curative-intent surgery or radiotherapy, including proPSMA and OSPREY findings.
- [Genetic and Epigenetic Background to Prostate Cancer Risk: Evidence Map](../papers/genetic-epigenetic-risk-prostate-cancer-evidence/index.md) — germline and tumour genetic context relevant to hereditary risk, Ashkenazi Jewish/APOE/BRCA clarification, aggressiveness prediction, family counselling, and precision-treatment questions.
