# 2026-07-29 — High-risk nonmetastatic treatment vs watchful waiting discussion

## Why this update was added

A patient-facing evidence note was added to address the argument that, because PIVOT and ProtecT found low prostate-cancer mortality with observation/monitoring in localized prostate cancer, intervention adds no value until metastatic spread is visible—even for Gleason 7 or high-risk cases.

## Main addition

- [High-Risk Nonmetastatic Prostate Cancer: Does Watchful Waiting Add Up?](../papers/high-risk-nonmetastatic-localized-treatment-evidence/index.md)

## Bottom line

The new evidence page concludes that **PIVOT and ProtecT are important cautions against overtreatment**, especially in low-risk and selected favourable intermediate-risk disease, but they **do not prove that watchful waiting is a safe default for high-risk nonmetastatic prostate cancer**.

For high-risk localized or locally advanced disease with no visible metastases, the discussion should account for:

- limitations of PIVOT and ProtecT for high-risk subgroup inference;
- increased metastasis/progression with monitoring in ProtecT;
- long-term SPCG-4 randomized evidence that radical prostatectomy reduced prostate-cancer death compared with watchful waiting in clinically detected localized disease;
- SPCG-7/SFUO-3 evidence that adding radiotherapy to endocrine therapy improved prostate-cancer-specific and overall survival in locally advanced/high-risk M0 disease;
- STAMPEDE evidence that abiraterone-based intensification improved metastasis-free and overall survival in high-risk nonmetastatic disease;
- guideline framing from AUA/ASTRO and EAU.

## Sources cited in the new page

- PIVOT 2012 and extended follow-up: Wilt et al., *NEJM* 2012; Wilt et al., *NEJM* 2017; Wilt et al., *European Urology* 2020.
- ProtecT 10- and 15-year outcomes: Hamdy et al., *NEJM* 2016 and 2023.
- SPCG-4 29-year follow-up: Bill-Axelson et al., *NEJM* 2018.
- SPCG-7/SFUO-3: Widmark et al., *Lancet* 2009.
- STAMPEDE high-risk nonmetastatic abiraterone analysis: Attard et al., *Lancet* 2022.
- proPSMA staging trial: Hofman et al., *Lancet* 2020.
- AUA/ASTRO and EAU current online localized prostate-cancer guidance.
